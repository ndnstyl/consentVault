11. Policies

Route: /policies, /policies/new, /policies/[version], /policies/[version]/approve · Roles: Admin (draft, approve); Compliance viewer and Developer (read)

Job: Let an admin draft the rules that turn evidence status into proceed or hold, and approve them with a recorded attestation.

Reads and writes: POST /v1/policies, POST /v1/policies/{version}:approve (contracts/admin.md). Gaps: GET list and one version, templates, attestation text read, rules schema, policy_superseded error, rule-sets vs policies naming.

Low fidelity, grayscale. Spec of record: ../handoff.md. With scripts off, every state shows stacked.
