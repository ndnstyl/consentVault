15. Connected systems and deliveries

Route: /systems, /systems/new, /systems/[system_id] · Roles: Admin (write, redrive); Developer (write, proposed); Compliance viewer (read)

Job: Connect the CRM, dialer, texting, email and AI agent systems that must receive every stop, see what was delivered, and redrive what failed.

Reads and writes: POST/PATCH /v1/systems, POST /v1/deliveries:redrive (contracts/admin.md), POST /v1/deliveries:query (contracts/suppression-sync.md). Gaps: GET list and detail, test push route and answer, per-system dead list and counts, inbound signal list.

Low fidelity, grayscale. Spec of record: ../handoff.md. With scripts off, every state shows stacked.

State: Default
