Leave ConsentVault

When an organization leaves, in this order:

  1. A final, complete evidence export is built and delivered to you.
  2. All credentials stop working.
  3. After the waiting period in your contract (at least 30 days), your encryption key is destroyed. Your records become unreadable everywhere, including backups.
  4. You receive a destruction record.

An active legal hold delays key destruction for what it covers.
