23. Records

Route: /records (filters in the query string; never a contact point) · Roles: Admin, Compliance viewer (full on reveal), Developer (fingerprints only)

Job: See every contact point the organization holds, filter by evidence status per channel and purpose, source, date and stop, and open one into its timeline.

Reads and writes: GET /v1/records, GET /v1/records/summary, POST /v1/records:search, POST /v1/records:reveal, POST /v1/records/exports (contracts/records.md, proposed)

Low fidelity, grayscale. Spec of record: ../handoff.md (section "Additions 2026-10-30"). With scripts off, every state shows stacked.

State: Default (admin, masked)
